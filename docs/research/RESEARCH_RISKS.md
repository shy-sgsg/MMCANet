## Proxy-target and latent-SAR optical gains have no known ordering (2026-10-25)

For `T=X+E`, the squared-error population Bayes gains from adding optical input differ by `E||δ_E||²+2E⟨δ_X,δ_E⟩`, where the increments are conditional-mean changes given `(Y,O)` versus `Y`. The local LR is generated from the same preprocessed noisy target, and no residual-independence or zero-increment assumption is established. Therefore neither the sign nor size of proxy-target gain identifies clean-SAR utility; finite-network metrics add further model/optimization error. No local correction factor follows. See [D-2026-10-25-152](DECISION_LOG.md#d-2026-10-25-152--record-the-exact-noisy-pseudo-target-bayes-risk-decomposition) and the [derivation](optical_information_analysis.md#exact-bayes-risk-decomposition-for-a-noisy-pseudo-target-2026-10-25).

Noise2Noise states its indexed clean-target condition in Eq. 6; in population notation, noisy-target and clean-target squared-loss estimators align when E[Z|X]=E[S|X], where target noise has zero conditional mean given S and is independent of X given S. The local shared-source pipeline has not been shown to meet that condition. Keep this as a target-validity risk, not evidence for or against optical utility; see D-2026-10-25-178 and D-2026-10-25-177.
